# Meaning through time

Choose a motion mechanism after stating what changed. Read or run a relevant
specimen before copying its movement. A specimen is a proposal with limits.

## Four independent decisions

| Grammar | Question | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State | What happened? | create, select, regroup, strengthen, revise, revert |
| Spatial | What relationship can movement express? | toward, apart, inside, anchored, release |
| Temporal | How does the change unfold? | immediate, continuous, staggered, settle, interrupt |
| Epistemic | What status does the display assert? | observed, inferred, provisional, contested, stale |

For each significant transition, state its verb, initiator, spatial origin,
reversibility, and whether feedback confirms or teaches an action. Preserve
object identity, selected state, and focus as the contract requires. State the
forbidden inference. Then choose an expressive personality and small project
vocabulary for timing, easing, intensity, rest, and supporting motion.

Do not treat a verb-to-animation mapping as a universal law. A revised heading
estimate and a physical turn can share geometry while asserting different facts.
Show the revision as a model event, without fabricating intermediate bearings.

## Observe the whole transition

Capture before, first response, early, middle, late, and settled states. Include
interrupted, reversed, and reduced-motion outcomes where supported. Use the
application's real state/replay seam. A single seekable timeline is useful for
an isolated specimen; it is not required architecture for a production app.

A deterministic query selects a frozen state. Record its self-reported state
and the actual capture clock brackets. A requested timestamp is not proof of
an observed frame time. Contact sheets reveal temporal structure but cannot prove
smoothness, control, or comprehension. Exercise the live controls as well.

During interruption, continue from the displayed state and preserve the action's
meaning. Test reversal before settlement and repeated input. Under reduced motion,
retain the relationship, result, focus, and available action without requiring
the viewer to watch an animation. Every frozen frame must carry essential meaning.

## Learn from a recording

Use an authorized local recording or runnable reference. Keep its source, license,
viewport, frame rate, crop, and uncertainty. Track the same object across sampled
frames; record timestamps and position, size, or opacity in actual units.
Separate object motion from camera/scroll movement and capture jitter.

The optional `motion-fit` helper compares sampled `time_ms,value` data with a few
simple curves. It reports residuals, not a recovered physical model. Sparse
samples cannot establish a spring or hidden easing function. Keep the raw samples
and alternate fits. Reimplement the meaningful portion, record it again, and
compare timing, geometry, interruption, and semantic invariants.

Reject an attractive reference when it communicates the wrong event. Preserve
emotional intent independently of the transition's factual meaning.
