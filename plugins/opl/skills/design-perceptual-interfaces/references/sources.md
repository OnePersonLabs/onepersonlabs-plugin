# Provenance and limits

This package restores and extends OPL's `design-perceptual-interfaces` skill.
It was introduced in `9fb7b438ee609aff048c770b27519bec270f9289` and removed in
`922706e01fe11e77a318b3c7b6cf19020ff86c9f`. The last pre-removal commit is
`d40bdd675308c9fe9867863d581c84ad72336d63`. The historical method and its channel
and interpretation references are preserved for inspection and comparison.
Only the archived method's relative reference links were adjusted for its new location.
Relative links in the relocated historical method were adjusted; its instructions are unchanged.
Removal is not evidence of an evaluated failure.

The user-supplied *Remedy Visual Design* conversation and *Building the NextUX
Design Borg* report motivated the visual-hypothesis, semantic-motion, observation,
and contextual-correction loop. Their private source copies stay outside the
shipping plugin. The historical Steady Helm image sequence supplied a concrete
example of image proposal, correction of unsupported claims, extraction of a
shared grammar, and request for an executable implementation. Generated geometry
and subsequent comprehension still require separate verification.
