## Compound Engineering

Adapted from EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin at revision
8673dfb96ad62c5e0cb6bc50fce312e7a66487a0:

- ce-pov and ce-bakeoff inform pov and develop-alternatives.
- ce-optimize supplies the optimization decision helper and workflow.
- ce-retune informs the A/A instruction-comparison guidance.
- ce-compound supplies the document-claim validator.
- ce-resolve-pr-feedback supplies the recoverable feedback-receipt helper.
