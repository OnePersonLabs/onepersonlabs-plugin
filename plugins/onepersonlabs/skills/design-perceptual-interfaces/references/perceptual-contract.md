# Meaning before representation

Use a contract when two different states could produce a consequentially wrong
reading. Keep a local craft correction to the few fields that distinguish it.
Put accepted meaning in the project's existing design record; do not duplicate it.

## Establish the work

Identify the user, decision, viewing conditions, attention, input constraints,
and consequence of a mistake. Separate observed facts, user intent, inference,
and supported advice. Preserve useful complexity at the right attention depth.
Ask only for information that determines a consequential unresolved choice.

For each dangerous confusion, name two real states that must read differently.
Include success, waiting, uncertain, stale, unavailable, conflict, and fault
states when they affect this interaction. A wide acceptable region and uncertainty
about the region's location have different meanings.

## Compact contract

```yaml
event: regroup
actual_change: grouping interpretation
unchanged: [object identity, content, selection, available actions]
communicate: the same objects are arranged through a different lens
never_imply: [content replaced, objects deleted, selection lost]
source_and_validity: current grouping model; stale output is marked
interaction:
  initiator: user
  reversible: true
  interruption: retarget from the displayed positions
without_motion: labels, stable IDs, and selection still identify the same objects
expression: continuous relocation with stable selected-object emphasis
evidence:
  semantic: identity, content, and selection invariants in the running UI
  comprehension: ask an unbriefed observer what changed and where selection went
  aesthetic: compare coherent alternatives without condition labels
```

This is a worked shape, not a required schema. Add a coordinate frame, units,
baseline, source age, expiry, persistence, or recovery rule when it affects truth.
Mark proposals separately from accepted requirements.

## Derive the expression

Choose what registers at a glance, what inspection reveals, and what study needs.
Allocate channels consistently: position and angle for grounded relationships;
extent for a declared quantity; shape and line pattern for identity; contrast
for priority; text for ambiguity; motion for change. Color reinforces another cue.
See [channel grammar](perceptual-grammar.md).

Choose layout, type, scale, spacing, and timing after a candidate works at its
actual viewing size. Keep physical truth and stable baselines. Do not interpolate
a revised estimate through unsupported values for visual smoothness. Show quiet
success explicitly; absence of advice must not masquerade as success.

Preserve the user's controls when the representation adapts. A new grouping or
emphasis must not silently change gesture meaning, focus, or available recovery.
An expressive design may serve delight, atmosphere, play, or musicality. State
that purpose without inventing a productivity justification.
